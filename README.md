#Deadpool (2016)

> *"With great power comes great irresponsibility."* - **Deadpool**

## Main Cast
- **Ryan Reynolds** as *Wade Wilson / Deadpool*  
- **Morena Baccarin** as *Vanessa*  
- **Ed Skrein** as *Ajax*

---

## Plot Summary
After being diagnosed with cancer, **Wade Wilson** undergoes a dangerous experiment that gives him **superhuman healing powers** — but leaves him disfigured.  
He becomes the wisecracking anti-hero **Deadpool**, hunting down the man responsible for his transformation.
